AuroraGate Expense Categorization

Active

A Kaggle competition entry that classifies bank transactions into spending categories (Groceries, Transportation, Shopping, etc.) from raw fields like the merchant description, amount, and date. The pipeline cleans and vectorises transaction descriptions with character n-gram TF-IDF, engineers features from the date and amount, and feeds everything into a LightGBM classifier.

Cross-validated with stratified 4-fold CV, it lands around 91% accuracy. Currently sitting 3rd on the competition leaderboard.

archived
